## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, empathetic, and exceptionally credible environment for pet owners and verified service providers. Rooted in modern lifestyle aesthetics rather than juvenile tropes, it strikes an intentional balance between cheerful approachability and medical/financial-grade reliability. The emotional baseline centers on immediate reassurance: leaving a family member in another person's care requires deep institutional trust conveyed through clarity, calm layouts, and tactile warmth.

Drawing from a warm contemporary visual style, the interface relies on generous white-space, softened edges, rich earthy primaries, and tactile depth. Visual cues eschew cartoonish elements in favor of clean editorial presentation, human-centric photography, organic pill geometries, and sophisticated typography. The tactile feel is defined by cushioned touch targets, subtle ambient earth-toned shadows, and crisp semantic surfaces that signal safety and verified authenticity.

## Colors

The color palette is built upon a grounding foundation of warm neutrals complemented by nature-inspired chromatic accents. 

- **Primary (`#FF6B4A`)**: A vibrant terracotta coral used for primary calls-to-action, key promotional highlights, and brand identity touchpoints. Supported by `#FFEAE4` for containers and `#D94827` for active/pressed states.
- **Secondary (`#2D8A68`)**: A calming forest sage green signaling trust, verified statuses, health records, and successful outcomes. Paired with `#E6F5EF` for badge backgrounds and `#1E6148` for deep contrast text.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#F5A623`)**: A golden honey yellow designated for ratings, active booking progression, and cautionary warnings.
- **Neutrals**: Built on organic, warm undertones rather than cold grays to preserve intimacy:
  - Surface Default: `#FCFBF9` (Warm White)
  - Surface Subdued: `#F7F4EF` (Warm Cream)
  - Border Subdued: `#EFECE6`
  - Border Default: `#E2DDD6`
  - Text Muted: `#8C857B`
  - Text Secondary: `#3A352F`
  - Text Primary: `#1A1613` (Dark Charcoal)
- **Feedback Semantics**:
  - Success: `#2D8A68` (Container: `#E6F5EF`)
  - Warning: `#F5A623` (Container: `#FEF6E7`)
  - Critical / Error: `#E53935` (Container: `#FDEBEB`)
  - Info: `#2575FC` (Container: `#EBF3FF`)

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all display, body, and micro-copy tiers. Its geometric clarity, wide aperture, and softened joins provide immediate warmth while sustaining high legibility on mobile viewports.

Key hierarchy principles:
- **Display & Headings**: Reserved for onboarding statements, dashboard greetings, and provider profile headers. Negative tracking reduces optical loosening at large sizes.
- **Body Styles**: Standardized on 14px and 16px with proportional line heights (1.4–1.5x) to maintain effortless scannability through service checklists, medical logs, and messaging threads.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Rendered in uppercase or bold weights with positive tracking (`+0.04em`) to ensure verification indicators and metadata tags stand out at small scale.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere strictly to an 8pt base grid system (with a 4pt sub-grid for icons, input padding, and badge internal alignment). 

- **Mobile Viewports (under 600px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `16px` (`1rem`) outer margins and `16px` gutters. Critical actions remain pinned to a bottom safe area container (`48px` to `56px` height target).
- **Tablet & Split-Screen Viewports (600px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `24px` or `32px` margins and `24px` gutters, accommodating side-by-side booking and chat interfaces.
- **Rhythm & Stacking**: Section cards must observe a standard vertical gap of `16px`, with interior component padding calibrated at `16px` for compact cards and `20px` for featured provider showcases.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered warm surfaces paired with tinted ambient diffusion rather than standard neutral dropshadows. Shadow colors incorporate the dark neutral text tone (`#1A1613`) at low opacities to integrate seamlessly with warm cream backgrounds.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Background plane (`#FCFBF9`) and subdued paneling (`#F7F4EF`). Defined by hairline borders (`1px solid #E2DDD6`) with zero cast shadow.
- **Level 1 (Low / Subtle)**: `0 1px 3px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.04)`. Applied to list item cards, input fields on focus, and standard chips.
- **Level 2 (Medium / Interactive Cards)**: `0 4px 12px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.08), 0 2px 4px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.04)`. Applied to active booking cards, provider profile summaries, and floating datepickers.
- **Level 3 (High / Overlays & Prompts)**: `0 10px 24px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.12), 0 4px 8px rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.06)`. Applied to bottom action sheets, modal dialogs, and navigation floating bars.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped orientation (`roundedness: 3`). Soft, circular curvature telegraphs gentleness and approachability, dispelling cold corporate or clinical tones while preserving structured layout discipline.

- **Micro Shapes (`8px`)**: Form inputs, small thumbnail containers, and compact status chips.
- **Container Shapes (`16px` to `24px`)**: Content cards, service selector tiles, and modal bottom sheet tops.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`)**: Interactive buttons, segmented controls, badge containers, filter chips, and map travel-duration pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Coral**: Solid `#FF6B4A` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover/Pressed: `#D94827`. Elevation Level 1. Full pill (`9999px`) or `16px` radius depending on form placement.
- **Secondary Sage**: Solid `#2D8A68` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover/Pressed: `#1E6148`.
- **Outline**: Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#E2DDD6`, `#1A1613` text. Pressed state transitions to `#F7F4EF`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, `#FF6B4A` or `#1A1613` text. Used for secondary tertiary actions like "Skip" or inline calendar shifts.
- **Floating Action Button (FAB)**: Primary coral, Level 3 elevation, circular (`56px x 56px`), with standard icon centered.

### Form Controls
- **Inputs (Text, Email, Phone, Dropdown)**: `48px` height, background `#FCFBF9`, border `1px solid #E2DDD6`, radius `12px`.
  - **Focused**: Border `2px solid #FF6B4A`, subtle glow `0 0 0 3px #FFEAE4`.
  - **Error**: Border `1.5px solid #E53935`, background `#FDEBEB` at 20% opacity. Accompanying helper text in `#E53935`.
- **OTP Input**: Discrete square boxes (`48px x 56px`), radius `12px`, centered display typography.

### Status & Verification Badges
- **Pill Geometry**: `24px` height, `8px` horizontal padding, radius `9999px`. Composed of a leading `12px` icon, followed by `11px` semibold tracking label.
- **PetCare Trust Badges**:
  - *Verified Provider*: Sage green container (`#E6F5EF`), forest text (`#1E6148`), shield check icon.
  - *Identity / Background Checked*: Warm cream container (`#F7F4EF`), dark charcoal text (`#3A352F`), ID lock icon.
  - *Top Rated*: Soft gold container (`#FEF6E7`), amber text (`#C78210`), star icon.
- **Booking Flow Badges**:
  - *Requested*: Amber background (`#FEF6E7`), text `#C78210`.
  - *Confirmed / In-Progress*: Teal-tinted sage (`#E6F5EF`), text `#1E6148`.
  - *Completed*: Subtle gray (`#EFECE6`), text `#3A352F`.
  - *Canceled*: Rose red (`#FDEBEB`), text `#E53935`.

### Cards & Media Ratios
- **Base Card**: Solid white (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm White (`#FCFBF9`), `1px solid #E2DDD6`, radius `16px`, Level 1 elevation.
- **Media Aspect Ratios**:
  - Pet Profile Avatar: `1:1` circle or `16px` rounded square.
  - Pet Showcase Card: `4:3` rounded viewport with top corner radius matching parent card.
  - Provider Portfolio Gallery: `16:9` rounded landscape containers with subtle internal gradient overlays.
  - Document & Certification Previews: `3:4` portrait view with border stroke `#E2DDD6`.

### Map Visual Tokens
- **Provider Pin**: Circular marker (`40px x 40px`) with `#FF6B4A` ring, white core holding provider avatar or service category glyph. Elevation Level 2.
- **Service Radius**: `#FF6B4A` fill at 12% opacity with a dashed `1.5px` border in `#FF6B4A`.
- **Route Tracking**: `#2D8A68` solid line with `4px` width and smooth linecaps.
- **Travel Duration Pill**: Floating pill on map canvas, Level 3 elevation, `#1A1613` background with white text and coral distance icon.
